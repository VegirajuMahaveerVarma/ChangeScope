# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in ChangeScope a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive.
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Giving and accepting feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, issue trackers, pull requests, and community spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through a contact method listed in the repository profile. Do not post sensitive details publicly.

This document is an initial project policy and may be expanded as the community grows.
